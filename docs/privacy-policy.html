You can permanently delete your account and all associated data in one of two ways:

  • From the web, without installing the app: using the link below
Delete My Account →
Deletion permanently removes your identity (email/password) and all associated app data (progress, scores, messages). This action cannot be undone. If you no longer have access to your password, email info@devkom.com.tr from your account email address; we will process manual deletion requests within 30 days at the latest.
